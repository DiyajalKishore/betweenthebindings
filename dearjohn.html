dear john, i see it all now that it’s gone

there are moments that i wish i hated you. but there are memories that remind me why i am grateful that i do not.

i have tried with you so many times, i no longer remember what it is like to love you wholly and have you love me gently.

i should not speak to you. i know this is the right thing to do. after all, i have been trying to for many years now. yet, every time i think that i am finally capable of doing it, of letting you go, i wish that i did not need to. oh, how badly do i wish that i did not need to.

i have loved you for a very long time. my love for you is deep, never fleeting. you have a place in my heart that will always be yours — no one can take that away from you. not even me. my grief for you is constant, never changing.

thank you for loving me. especially on the days when i was difficult to love.

i wish i could forget the person you have become so i can remember who you were without feeling so guilty for loving you so much that i couldn’t care less if you broke my heart.

the heartbreak you have left me with is a heavy weight on my shoulders but i would carry this burden with me forever if it meant never needing to let you go.

eventually, you will forget all about me. the sigh that leaves my lips when you hold me close. the echo of my laughter intertwined with yours. the feel of my head resting against your beating heart.

but, one day, i hope you remember me.

i hope the need to touch me has you clenching your fists so tightly that you are reminded of what it felt like to hold me. i hope your soul yearns to dance with mine. i hope your heart stutters at the mention of my favourite colour.

i wish for you to carry my love with you. forever. i hope you are haunted by it on days when the cold creeps into your veins and you lose memory of my smile.

and, even after all this time, all this hurt, all this pain, my heart still yearns for your unwavering touch. the scars you have embedded in my bones still bleed at the trace of your sighs. despite the reminiscent feel of the cuts you have inflicted.

nostalgia eats at my heart till i am left starving for a love that i should not crave. a love that will not feed me. but starving people will eat anything.

experiencing love with you has been one of the most beautiful things i have had the privilege of enjoying. love with you was gentle. until it was not. love with you was angry. until it was not. love with you was patient. until it was not. love with you was all encompassing. until it was not.

love with you is eternal.

these hands had to let it go free. yet, all i have ever wanted was for this love to come back to me.

you know me like no other. please keep all my secrets safe.

with all my love that no longer belongs to you,

lae.
