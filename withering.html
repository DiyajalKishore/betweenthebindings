withering, waiting, wishing

darling, is it too late for you and i?

i’ll leave on the lights for you,
as the evening rain settles to dew.

i’ll leave on the lights for you,
as who you once loved fades out of view.

i’ll leave on the lights for you,
as i drown in a deep, scathing blue.

i’ll leave on the lights for you,
as i ruin it all over, like i always do.

i’ll leave on the lights for you,
regardless of the pain you have put me through.

i’ll leave on the lights for you,
as i can only hope you come home soon.
