the great lesbian debate

to dyke or not to dyke? #fagitout

growing up, my queerness was never a question.

i did not sit in silence surrounded by darkness and wonder about it until my eyes leaked acid rain and coldness creeped into my veins at the thought of what it could mean.

sexuality is a spectrum, for sure. safe to say, i have been on opposite ends of this seesaw for most of my life.

when i was twelve, i did not add a name to what i felt. it was just a feeling. something that i never resisted but it always lingered.

and boy, did it linger.
