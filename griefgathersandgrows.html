grief gathers and grows

do you think i have forgotten?

my house is haunted by the loss of you.
you linger in every corner,
in every nook and cranny,
in all the space above,
in all the space below,
and all the space in between.

i yearn for your presence silently,
and i miss the feel of you violently.

and on days when i feel as if i have forgotten,
the thought of you lurks beside me like
an old, familiar friend.

the ghost of who you once were consumes me whole.

your memory grasps at my very breath,
and refuses to let me sigh
without remembering the smile i have not smiled
since you left.

my love for you is deep,
never-fleeting.

my grief for you is constant,
never-changing.

The ideas overflow and the pen runs out of ink. The book is busting at the seams and the pages are worn out.

My heart is full of love and I am no longer starving.

Only when I am buried between the bindings.
