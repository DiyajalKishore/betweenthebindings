Jul 14, 2026•1 min read

my curses crumble between cries

i’ll ruin it all over, like i always do.

if you try to get too close,
i will bare my teeth and bite.
i will take and take, all the pieces you love most,
and after, i will cry into my pillow all night.

i will sit here with overflowing acid rain,
with my head hidden under stained covers,
and let my gut rot
with everything i’m too afraid to spill.
not even your smile’s tender crinkle
can undo my heart’s wrinkle,
and all the emptiness she has brought.

i will sit here,
alone,
and crumble
beneath the weight of unspoken words.
