tossing pennies

we used to kiss all night but now there's just no fuse

darling, is it too late for you and i?

come home,
let the beat of your heart linger on my fingertips.

come home,
let me mend all your broken bones.

come home,
leave your fears in the darkness beyond.

come home,
step into the light,
and bask in my warmth.

darling,
please,
come home.
