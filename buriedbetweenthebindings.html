buried between the bindings

when is my heart full and no longer starving?

In my 21 years of life, I cannot recall a single, conscious memory of there ever being a time when I did not have a book or pen in my hands.

Growing up, I found irrevocable comfort in between the pages of tattered, old books I borrowed from the library. Through these books, I had the opportunity to experience a type of privilege that other children my age did not care for. I discovered a magical ability that I never wanted to let go of. Something I never wanted to share.

It felt as if I found the pot of gold at the end of a rainbow. Through these books, I unearthed worlds that a younger me could not fathom being real. I no longer had to choose between being a princess or a pirate. I no longer had to imagine being a fairy or a knight riding into battle on a fantastic beast. I no longer had to pretend to be a normal teenager with a life so unlike mine. Through these books, I was able to be all these things at once.

I weaved my soul in between the printed words. I ripped my heart out of my chest and carefully placed it between the bindings.

One day, I realized that reading about these worlds were no longer enough. It was time to start creating my own.

When my eyes closed and my breaths evened out, I was transported to a place where the words moved quickly, and I had to be focused enough to track them down. I had to track down every letter, every space between words, every comma and full stop. There was no time to spare. It was a race against the sun rising and the moon disappearing. Every night, it was my mission to wrangle up these words and shove them into my back pocket. It was my duty to keep them safe and secure until I could find pen and paper.

Writing is my form of escape. When it is just my thoughts and I, peace flutters around the four walls of my bedroom. When my pen finds the page, I am free at last. When reality becomes unbearable, all I have to do is pull out my journal. All I have to do is close my eyes and visit the secret gardens in my mind.

The ideas overflow and the pen runs out of ink. The book is busting at the seams and the pages are worn out.

My heart is full of love and I am no longer starving.

Only when I am buried between the bindings.
