my heart is buried in your backyard

pushing it down and praying that one day she is unearthed once more, no longer in your possession, but held firmly, warmly, wholly in my own hands.

my brittle, broken heart begins to beat.
for the briefest of moments, i had thought that it never would,
and for the longest of times, i had thought that it never could.
but, it is oh so sensible for a love this sweet.

lose yourself in me,
leave your worries at the door.
wonder about all that can be,
as we intertwine our souls forevermore.

spring lingers, summer is near.
the loss of this love is my greatest fear.

here i am,
fragmented,
fractured,
finally fixed.

my battered, bruised heart begins to break.
for the briefest of moments, i had hoped that it never could,
and for the longest of times, i had hoped that it never would.
but now, i am filled with a never-ending, ever-prescent ache.

spread across the evergreen,
reminiscing on what could have been.
you bleed into my prose,
and haunt me as my eyes close.

summer lingers, winter is near.
the loss of this love is now here.

here i am,
bare-footed,
broken-boned,
buried in your backyard.
